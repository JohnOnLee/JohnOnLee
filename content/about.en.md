**John Lee** — a builder who makes and operates software and AI products. I imagine a future where anyone can turn an idea into a product, and own what they build as an asset.

Right now I'm building [Monet](https://github.com/team-monet/monet), an open-source memory platform for AI agents. The [AI Radar](/en/brief/) on this site is published daily by Hermes, an agent I built — what I learn from building goes straight back into running this site.

## This site

I write in three tracks.

- **[AI Radar](/en/brief/)** — spotting the shifts good builders can't afford to miss. Not the news, but what to pay attention to.
- **[Insights](/en/blog/)** — interpreting what changes mean and naming the problems that keep recurring. Judgment frames, not information.
- **[Guides & Tools](/en/guides/)** — turning that judgment into real systems and ways of working. Execution, not how-tos.

The writing is for vibe coders, professional developers, product designers, and founders — people turning ideas into real products. Rather than selling a formula, I separate what I have verified firsthand from what I am still testing.
