This site (john.onlee.io) is a personal site run by John Lee. There are no accounts, comments, or contact forms, and the site does not directly collect or store visitors' personal information. Below is what the tools used by this site collect.

## Traffic analytics (Google Analytics 4)

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how content is read. GA4 uses cookies and similar technologies to collect anonymized usage data such as page views, browser and device information, and approximate region. This data is not used to identify individual visitors.

See [how Google uses data from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and use the [GA opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) if you prefer not to be counted.

## Advertising (Google AdSense — planned)

Ads may appear in the future, limited to the [Guides & Tools](/en/guides/) section. When ads are served:

- Google and third-party vendors may use advertising cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- See [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) for details.

Ads will not appear in other sections (AI Radar, Insights).

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies does not limit your ability to read anything on this site.

## External links

Posts on this site link to external sites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

## Changes

Any changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

## Contact

For questions about this site, please open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/JohnOnLee/JohnOnLee/issues).
