## OpenAI has paused new Pro sign-ups under heavy Astra demand
- **The $200 Pro plan is temporarily closed to new users**: OpenAI says Astra demand is straining its infrastructure. Existing Pro access and the API, Go, and Plus plans remain available. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/10/openai-puts-pro-subscriptions-on-hold-due-to-astra-demand/)

## Check actual access before building a product promise around Astra
- **Model quality is only part of the dependency**: The API is still open, so this is not a service outage. Before shipping an Astra-based feature, confirm that the model remains available in each account and region under the plans your product uses.

## Run your core flow on a second model
- **Compare representative requests across both models**: Measure task success, latency, and cost on Astra and a fallback. If the gap is small, automate the switch. If it is large, tell users clearly that the feature depends on Astra.

## The duration of the pause and any API impact remain unknown
- **Do not treat today's capacity as settled**: OpenAI has not disclosed how long sign-ups will remain closed or the size of the demand surge. The API and lower-cost plans remain open, but the same pressure could lead to later usage limits.
