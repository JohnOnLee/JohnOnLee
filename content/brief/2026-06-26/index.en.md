## Key Shifts
- **OpenAI delays GPT-5.6 after Trump administration request**: The White House asked OpenAI to stagger its next major model release over security concerns. Sam Altman told employees in a company Q&A that GPT-5.6 would ship as a limited preview to select enterprise customers, with the administration approving access on a case-by-case basis — a notably softer deal than what rival Anthropic received. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/957372/openai-will-delay-gpt-5-6-after-trump-administration-request)

## AI Future Signals
- **Ford had to hire back former engineers to fix AI automation mistakes**: Celebrating a JD Power quality ranking win, Ford revealed that its automated production and design systems were less robust than assumed — forcing the company to bring back retired engineers to correct errors. A textbook case that enterprise AI effectiveness depends entirely on training data quality. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/transportation/956316/ford-quality-jd-power-ranking-ai-automated-mistakes)
- **AI adoption is reshaping startup org structures, not just products**: Amperity's layoffs were not just cost-cutting — they reflected a fundamental shift in how the company operates as AI tools took over specific functions. A signal that every organization adopting AI needs to rethink workforce design, not just workflow automation. [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/amperity-hit-with-layoffs-as-ai-changes-the-shape-of-the-customer-data-startup-and-how-it-operates/)
