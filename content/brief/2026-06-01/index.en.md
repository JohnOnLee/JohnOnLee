## AI Future Signals
- **Wearable AI is becoming the next platform war**: Meta's AI pendant, Apple's AI glasses, and their divergent strategies (Meta: voice assistant + camera, Apple: fashion-first eyewear) show AI interaction moving beyond the smartphone. Evidence: three major platform companies — Meta, Apple, and NVIDIA — all signaled hardware ambitions in the same week.
- **Data centers are the new environmental justice battleground**: Erin Brockovich has begun publicly tracking AI data center construction practices and organizing community pushback. As AI infrastructure scales, local political resistance will become a material risk factor for expansion. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/31/erin-brockovich-takes-aim-at-data-center-secrecy/)
- **1-bit quantization brings generative AI to local devices**: Bonsai Image 4B demonstrates a 4B-parameter image generation model running locally via 1-bit quantization. The gap between cloud-dependent and on-device AI is closing faster than most realize. [PRISM ML](https://prismml.com/news/bonsai-image-4b)
- **Usage-based pricing is becoming the norm for AI tools**: GitHub Copilot's controversial token billing is one data point in a broader shift from flat-rate subscriptions to measured consumption. Enterprise AI unit economics are starting to crystallize — this will reshape how AI products are packaged and sold.

## Realistic Opportunities / Experiments
- **Build ultra-lightweight services optimized for wearable AI**: With Meta pendant and Apple glasses arriving 2027-2028, now is the time to experiment with voice-first micro-apps: real-time summarization/translation, context-aware memory, heads-up micro-dashboards. The interaction paradigm will be fundamentally different from phone apps.
- **Turn AI agents' "no-sudo" creativity into a product story**: A viral HN post showed Codex finding clever workarounds when denied sudo access. "AI that works even in constrained enterprise environments" is a compelling GTM narrative. Test and document what your AI agents can do under tight permission boundaries — it's a differentiator.
