## Meta's first frontier model keeps its strongest tier off the API

Meta's Superintelligence Lab released Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full on October 12, its first frontier model. It runs on the Meta Model API at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens. Input can be text, image, speech or video. [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/10/introducing-muse-wonder-models/) [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/12/meta-releases-its-first-frontier-model-muse-wonder/)

The stronger of the two is not sold by the token at all. Meta sells the Full variant only through a monthly subscription to Muse Code, its coding agent, and never on the API. Muse Code runs $50 or $200 a month, and the Muse app itself costs $20 or $100. One family. Two payment paths. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/12/meta-muse-wonder-ai-model-agent.html)

Artificial Analysis scores Muse Wonder at 52 on its Intelligence Index and the Full variant at 56, which ranks it fourth of 699 models. Meta's own model card claims benchmark scores comparable to Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra, but those are the vendor's numbers. [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/muse-wonder)

## The API-reachable model is the only one your product can call

For an indie builder, what changed is procurement. If your plan is to route your product to whichever model scores highest on the leaderboards, that plan now has a hole in it, and the hole sits at the top of the family. The one in fourth place is not something your app can invoke at any price. Assume the top tier is always on the token menu, and this week proves you wrong. When capability follows the subscription tier, someone else's price list sets your ceiling.

Muse Wonder is the variant worth testing, and the claim to check is memory. The company says Active Memory carries what matters across conversations, which would cut down the save-summarize-reinject code that plenty of products hand-roll. For now that is a description, not a measured result. Take a few tasks your product already runs, point both models at them, and see whether your own context plumbing gets shorter.
