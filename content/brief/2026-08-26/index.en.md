## Startup / Product / Platform Radar
- **Apple M6 and M5 Ultra: a big lift for on-device AI compute**: Apple unveiled the M6/M5 Ultra silicon alongside Mac Studio and Mac mini (M6/M5 Pro), touting a large jump in AI compute — a useful baseline for teams shipping local inference. [Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-m6-and-m5-ultra-for-a-big-leap-in-performance-and-ai-compute/) · [Apple Mac Studio](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-new-mac-studio-with-m5-max-and-m5-ultra/)
- **JetBrains Junie Local: a fully on-device coding agent for Macs**: JetBrains shipped Junie Local, its coding agent running entirely on-device on macOS — relevant for teams that can't let code leave their infrastructure. [Techstrong.ai](https://techstrong.ai/articles/jetbrains-releases-junie-local-bringing-its-coding-agent-fully-on-device-to-macs/)
- **Perplexity's 'Portable Computer': a local-first AI device**: Perplexity unveiled a privacy-forward, local-first 'Portable Computer', pushing a vision where personal data stays on-device. [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/introducing-portable-computer-for-local-first-ai)
- **Google launches Gemini Enterprise for Legal**: Google Cloud expanded its vertical AI line with a legal-specific enterprise offering — a sign of domain-focused AI products maturing. [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/introducing-gemini-enterprise-for-legal)
- **Efficient open MoE 'Qwen 3.8-Flash-Next' (125B a6B) release imminent**: Alibaba's Qwen team is set to release a sparse-MoE open model — an attractive low-cost fine-tuning option for cost- and privacy-sensitive teams. [ModelScope](https://modelscope.cn/models/Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next)

[Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-m6-and-m5-ultra-for-a-big-leap-in-performance-and-ai-compute/) · [Techstrong.ai](https://techstrong.ai/articles/jetbrains-releases-junie-local-bringing-its-coding-agent-fully-on-device-to-macs/)
