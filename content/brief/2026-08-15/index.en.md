## Startup / Product / Platform Radar
- **SpaceX completes its Cursor acquisition — a $60B all-stock deal**: The process that began in April is done. Cursor gains access to the world's largest GPU fleet, in what a16z calls the largest venture-backed startup acquisition ever. [Cursor](https://cursor.com/blog/joining-spacex) · [a16z](https://www.a16z.news/p/cursor-spacexai-fastest-iterating-team)
- **China's Zhipu ships GLM-5.3, its "strongest open-weights coding model"**: Same base as GLM-5.2, with all gains from extended post-training; weights go open source in two weeks after a security review. [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/zhipu-ai-releases-glm-5-3-claims-its-the-strongest-open-weights-coding-model/)
- **Meta gives up control of AI startup Manus after China blocked the deal**: The acquisition was reversed after Chinese regulators cited export controls; Manus returns to independence and must delete some data gathered under Meta. [Computerworld](https://www.computerworld.com/article/4209728/meta-gives-up-control-of-chinese-ai-startup-manus-after-eight-months.html)
- **French startup Kog squeezes more inference out of existing GPUs**: Software optimization on AMD MI300X and Nvidia H200 hardware; the solo founder reports 200 business leads, with software engineering as the first use case. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/14/kog-is-going-deeper-to-squeeze-more-inference-out-of-gpus/)
- **Google open-sources HEIR, a compiler for private AI via homomorphic encryption**: A new tool in the Private Computing Toolkit that unlocks cryptographically-secure inference. [Google](https://blog.google/security/how-google-is-making-private-ai-practical-with-homomorphic-encryption/)

## AI Future Signals
- **Compute owners are buying the application layer**: SpaceX acquiring Cursor shows frontier-compute holders moving to own the coding-tool "killer app." [a16z](https://www.a16z.news/p/cursor-spacexai-fastest-iterating-team) · [Cursor](https://cursor.com/blog/joining-spacex)
- **Open-weight models gain "cyber" capability**: GLM-5.3 was trained to find vulnerabilities and now reasons across "multiple stages of exploitation" — a new variable in open-model security. [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/zhipu-ai-releases-glm-5-3-claims-its-the-strongest-open-weights-coding-model/)
- **Inference on encrypted data becomes practical**: Homomorphic encryption is moving from slow research to a usable open-source compiler, opening the door to privacy-preserving AI products. [Google](https://blog.google/security/how-google-is-making-private-ai-practical-with-homomorphic-encryption/)

## Realistic Opportunities / Experiments
- **The gap for neutral, self-hosted coding tools**: With Cursor inside SpaceX, teams wanting an ecosystem-neutral option can lean on open-weight models like GLM-5.3. [a16z](https://www.a16z.news/p/cursor-spacexai-fastest-iterating-team) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/zhipu-ai-releases-glm-5-3-claims-its-the-strongest-open-weights-coding-model/)
- **An app layer that infers over encrypted data**: Health, finance, and legal products are natural experiments for HEIR-style privacy-preserving inference. [Google](https://blog.google/security/how-google-is-making-private-ai-practical-with-homomorphic-encryption/)
- **Fix latency bottlenecks in software, not silicon**: Kog's approach shows teams can unlock speed and cost gains on GPUs they already own for latency-sensitive agents. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/14/kog-is-going-deeper-to-squeeze-more-inference-out-of-gpus/)

## Uncertainties / Keep Watching
- **Cursor's independence and neutrality**: Whether Cursor stays model-neutral or reorients around SpaceXAI models is an open question. [a16z](https://www.a16z.news/p/cursor-spacexai-fastest-iterating-team)
- **GLM-5.3's cyber capability, once open-sourced**: A model that plans "complete exploitation chains" could aid offense as well as defense when the weights drop in two weeks. [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/zhipu-ai-releases-glm-5-3-claims-its-the-strongest-open-weights-coding-model/)
