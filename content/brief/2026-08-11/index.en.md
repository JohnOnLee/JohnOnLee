## Startup / Product / Platform Radar
- **Meta releases Muse Glimmer, a 30B open on-device agent model**: Meta Superintelligence Labs launched an open-weight agentic model optimized for always-on local execution on consumer hardware. Runs agent workflows entirely on-device with no cloud dependency. [Meta AI Research](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-glimmer-open-agentic-model) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/10/metas-new-glimmer-ai-model-offers-a-hint-at-zuckerbergs-personal-intelligence-vision/)
- **Docker launches Sandboxes for AI coding agents**: Disposable, isolated execution environments for agents to safely run and test code. Signals growing investment in agent-native infrastructure. [Docker](https://www.docker.com/products/docker-sandboxes/)
- **Stoa Markets (YC S26) launches GPU and AI server marketplace**: A B2B marketplace for buying and selling AI hardware — GPUs, AI servers, and related compute. [Stoa](https://www.stoaexchange.com)
- **Discovered Materials raises $9M for AI-driven chip materials discovery**: Uses ML to find novel materials enabling more efficient, cooler-running semiconductors. Addresses the thermal management bottleneck in chip design. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/10/discovered-materials-is-playing-ai-whack-a-mole-to-hunt-cooler-chips/)

The sharpest open-weight salvo yet against closed-model competitors. [Meta](https://www.meta.com/thefutureisforeveryone/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/10/mark-zuckerbergs-ai-manifesto-is-exactly-why-people-dont-like-ai/)

## Realistic Opportunities / Experiments
- **On-device agent models unlock new product categories**: A 30B model running on consumer hardware means privacy-preserving personal assistants, local AI copilots, and edge-native agent products are now viable. Teams building for edge devices should evaluate open-weight models as a differentiation strategy. [Meta AI Research](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-glimmer-open-agentic-model)
- **AI agent infrastructure is becoming a distinct layer**: Docker Sandboxes and Stoa Markets landing the same day illustrate how agent-native infrastructure — execution environments, compute marketplaces, evaluation frameworks — is coalescing into its own ecosystem. Opportunity exists in building tooling around the agent lifecycle. [Docker](https://www.docker.com/products/docker-sandboxes/) · [Stoa](https://www.stoaexchange.com)
