## OpenAI put Sponsored Agents inside ChatGPT ads and wired HubSpot and Shopify into the buy flow
- **Sponsored Agents beta**: a ChatGPT user who clicks an ad can start a conversation with an agent the business sponsors. OpenAI began testing with a group of US advertisers on September 16. [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-chatgpt-sponsored-agents-ads-manager-hubspot-shopify)
- **"A clearly labeled conversation"**: the thread is distinct from ChatGPT's independent answers and separate from the conversation the user started. Users can ask follow-ups, then follow a link to the advertiser's site. [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-chatgpt-sponsored-agents-ads-manager-hubspot-shopify)
- **Beta eligibility**: participation can carry conditions such as creative automation and automatic bidding, plus a minimum daily ad spend for 28 days. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/form/sponsored-agents-hubspot/)
- **Ads Manager plugin**: advertisers create campaigns, update them and analyse performance with plain-language prompts. The tool reads a landing page and a campaign objective, suggests copy and images, and leaves the edits to the advertiser. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/reimagining-advertising-with-ai/) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/openai-tests-advertiser-sponsored-agents-expands-ai-tools-chatgpt-ads-2026-09-16/)
- **AI copy customisation**: existing headlines and descriptions get rewritten for the context of a conversation and translated into the user's preferred language. [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-chatgpt-sponsored-agents-ads-manager-hubspot-shopify)
- **HubSpot is the first CRM partner, Shopify the first ecommerce partner**: from September 16, a HubSpot account can hold a ChatGPT Ads account, so campaign creation and performance reporting sit next to lead follow-up in one place. [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-chatgpt-sponsored-agents-ads-manager-hubspot-shopify)
- **The Shopify app and the market schedule**: US merchants run ads straight from products already connected through Shopify Catalog; the app reaches other ChatGPT Ads markets on September 23. [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-chatgpt-sponsored-agents-ads-manager-hubspot-shopify)
- **How the channel got here**: cost-per-click pricing in April, a $1bn annualised run rate on August 31, and an Amazon advertiser pilot on September 11. [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-chatgpt-sponsored-agents-ads-manager-hubspot-shopify)

## When the click lands in a conversation instead of a landing page, your product's front door moves
- **Attaching a channel got cheaper**: a Shopify seller has the catalog connected already, so the app is the whole integration. Nobody rebuilds a stack to test one more channel.
- **Copy stops being the bottleneck**: drafts of headlines and images arrive from the landing page, which moves the work from writing to reviewing and measuring.
- **The first screen becomes the first answer**: if a conversation opens after the click, the hero headline is not what decides the conversion.
- **Tools you already run become ad surfaces**: campaigns and reporting inside HubSpot and Shopify mean the team already living there never leaves it to buy ads.
- **Product data an agent can answer from turns into an asset**: an agent that reads clean product data answers better, so specs and shipping details decide how the ad performs.

## What to try now: design the conversation that sits behind the click
- **Build one agent that answers for your product**: load product data and your most common questions, then run it. What the ad promises but the agent cannot answer shows up fast.
- **Run generated copy against your own**: take the variants that come out of one landing page and put them next to yours, then compare click-through and conversion.
- **Fix three questions you will always get**: pick the ones buyers ask every time. Dimensions and compatibility are the usual ones, so put the answers in the agent.
- **Test a conversational landing page**: point one ad at a screen that opens with a few questions and another at the current page, then compare where people drop off.

## The line between a labeled ad and ChatGPT's own answer is still untested
- **The label may not carry the trust**: a clearly marked sponsored thread can still be read as ChatGPT's own answer.
- **Performance data is thin**: where a conversation ends and whether a lead justifies the spend needs advertiser data that does not exist yet.
- **Click prices can climb**: when advertisers pile into a new surface, cost per click rises and the cheap window closes.
- **The schedule outside the US is unsettled**: the Shopify app arrives in other markets on September 23, so sellers elsewhere should check their own date.
