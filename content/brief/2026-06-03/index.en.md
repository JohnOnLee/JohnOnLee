## AI Future Signals
- **AI-native security auditing becomes infrastructure**: That Mythos found a 27-year-old OpenBSD vulnerability implies vast undiscovered flaws remain in the software we all depend on. Within 1–2 years, continuous AI-driven security auditing of all critical infrastructure will be standard, not optional
- **End of the "free money" AI budget era**: Uber is the canary. Copilot's usage-pricing backlash signals that both enterprises and developers are realizing AI costs far exceed initial expectations. The real barrier to AI adoption won't be technical capability — it'll be economics
- **Always-on agents become the new OS layer**: Scout's "autopilot" concept — agents working in the background without being prompted — combined with Google Spark and OpenAI Codex, represents a battle for the agent OS layer. Whoever wins this layer owns the next decade's platform
- **AI Cold War blocs forming**: Project Glasswing (Anthropic + US Big Tech alliance) vs. China (DeepSeek). What began in cybersecurity is evolving into geopolitical alignment around AI standards, infrastructure access, and allied-nation lines

## Realistic Opportunities / Experiments
- **AI cost optimization middleware**: Every company with AI spend needs what Uber just scrambled to build — usage monitoring, model routing (expensive vs. cheap models), budget alerts, team-level quotas. Copilot billing backlash confirms market timing is right
- **Vertical agents with hard ROI metrics**: Vague "AI boosts productivity" claims are dead. Build agents for narrow domains where cost savings or revenue lift is directly measurable — construction (Rudus), manufacturing, logistics. The differentiator is the number, not the demo
- **AI security audit SaaS for the mid-market**: Mythos-level capability is out of reach, but there's a gap below the enterprise/government tier. Mid-size companies can't afford a Glasswing partnership but still need vulnerability scanning — a SaaS play with clear per-audit pricing

## Uncertainties / Keep Watching
- **Will Anthropic's IPO reprice the entire AI sector?** $1T valuation may hold in private markets but public markets are less forgiving
- **Is Uber an outlier or the leading edge of an enterprise AI spending correction?** If it's the latter, Copilot, Cursor, Codex revenue models face a reckoning
- **Could Project Glasswing evolve into a de facto AI security alliance?** If cybersecurity expands into AI standards and regulation, non-participating companies and nations risk exclusion
- **How durable is the 'no-AI' backlash?** DuckDuckGo's surge, data center moratoria, Harvard commencement rhetoric — temporary reaction or a lasting consumer segment?
