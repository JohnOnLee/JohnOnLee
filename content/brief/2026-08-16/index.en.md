## Startup / Product / Platform Radar
- **Nvidia halves its OpenAI data-center backstop**: Per a WSJ exclusive, Nvidia is cutting its planned $250 billion guarantee for OpenAI's Ohio data center to roughly the low $100 billions, under investor pressure — an early sign that AI-infrastructure financing is being re-priced. [WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/tech/nvidia-downsizes-plans-for-250-billion-guarantee-of-openai-data-center-b56c38d3) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/nvidia-scales-back-250-billion-openai-data-center-guarantee-wsj-reports-2026-08-14/)
- **Anthropic explains how Claude's new text watermarks work**: The company says the EU-driven watermarking works by adjusting the choice of "inconsequential words" in generated text, turning content-provenance tracing into a shipping product feature. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/15/anthropic-shares-more-details-about-how-claudes-new-watermarks-will-work/)
- **Google now lets users remove visible watermarks from AI generations**: The opposite of Anthropic's approach — Google lets users strip the visible watermark from AI images and video, showing how provenance policies are diverging across platforms. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/14/google-will-now-allow-users-to-remove-visible-watermark-from-its-ai-generations/)
- **Apple is training its own China AI model with Alibaba's help**: Apple is reportedly building a China-specific model with Alibaba and won unprecedented Beijing clearance — the first foreign firm to do so, marking a shift in how Western companies enter the China market. [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/14/apples-china-ai-strategy-now-includes-training-its-own-custom-model-per-report/)
