## Startup / Product / Platform Radar

- **Etched doubles its valuation to $21B in under a month**: The AI inference chip startup raised another $700 million in a round led by Jane Street, doubling its valuation to $21 billion. Etched says Jane Street led the round after installing the startup's first shipped AI cluster. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/etcheds-valuation-doubles-to-21b-in-a-month/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/18/inference-chip-startup-etched-raises-another-700m-at-21b-valuation/)

- **Anthropic nears a multibillion-dollar deal for Israeli AI startup Decart**: Anthropic is in advanced talks to acquire the Israeli AI startup Decart in a multibillion-dollar deal. Decart shareholders see more upside in Anthropic stock, given Anthropic is planning the largest IPO in history at a $2 trillion valuation. [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/business-and-innovation/tech-and-start-ups/article-905834)

- **Velaura AI raises a $110M Series A at a $1B valuation**: The Santa Clara company's Titan Core chip platform promises a 2-4x improvement in performance per watt for AI workloads. StepStone Group, Samsung Catalyst Fund, and Maverick Silicon joined the round. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/velaura-ai-raises-110m-series-140115866.html)

- **Mark Cuban-backed 'physical AI' startup nears a $500M SPAC deal**: A startup backed by Mark Cuban that makes hardware and software to more safely control and operate autonomous robots plans to go public via a blank-check vehicle at a $500 million valuation. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/mark-cuban-backed-physical-ai-100025916.html)

## AI Future Signals

[Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/physical-ai-funding-startups-robotics-aerospace-h1-2026/)

- **Unitree unveils a humanoid robot faster than Usain Bolt**: The Chinese robotics company's 'Superman' robot jumped two metres from standing and reached 12.66 metres per second — faster than the record-holder if sustained over 100 metres. [AFR](https://www.afr.com/world/asia/faster-than-usain-bolt-china-s-unitree-unveils-superman-robot-20260818-p60pd5)

- **Reach Capital closes a $265M Fund V for AI founders**: Reach Capital announced an oversubscribed $265 million Fund V to back AI founders building to 'expand human potential' — a reminder that early-stage capital keeps flowing even as mega-rounds dominate headlines. ## Realistic Opportunities / Experiments

- **Performance-per-watt is becoming the inference battleground**: With Etched and Velaura both scaling on dedicated inference silicon and power efficiency, energy cost is now a core axis of competition for AI workloads. Teams building tools to measure and optimize inference cost and energy have a real opening. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/velaura-ai-raises-110m-series-140115866.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/etcheds-valuation-doubles-to-21b-in-a-month/)

- **The robotics safety-and-control software layer**: The Cuban-backed startup's safe-control hardware/software and Unitree's speed race both signal that as robots get faster, safety and verification become the bottleneck. Fleet safety certification and simulation-testing tools are an emerging niche. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/mark-cuban-backed-physical-ai-100025916.html) · [AFR](https://www.afr.com/world/asia/faster-than-usain-bolt-china-s-unitree-unveils-superman-robot-20260818-p60pd5)
