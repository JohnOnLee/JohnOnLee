## Startup / Product / Platform Radar

- **Stripe acquires OpenRouter, the AI model routing hub**: Stripe is acquiring OpenRouter, the AI model marketplace and routing layer. OpenRouter says it keeps the same mission, name, product, and roadmap, continuing independent neutral routing. The payments giant now controls access to and routing across frontier models. [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/openrouter-is-joining-stripe/) · [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49364559)

- **Rillet raises a $100M Series C at a $1B valuation**: Two years after emerging from stealth, Rillet — which automates financial close and reporting with AI — hit unicorn status with a $100M Series C. Capital and attention keep concentrating on AI-driven finance and accounting workflows. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/rillet-raises-100m-series-c-at-1b-valuation-2-years-after-emerging-from-stealth/)

- **Ex-Nvidia lab leader Sanja Fidler launches Veeda AI for world models**: Former Nvidia research lead Sanja Fidler is founding Veeda AI, focused on modeling the physical world via world models. Moving beyond perception and reasoning toward models that predict the world is becoming a distinct investment and research theme. [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/former-nvidia-lab-leader-sanja-fidler-launches-veeda-ai-to-tackle-world-models/)

- **Calendly enters the AI meeting-note-taker market**: The scheduling platform is joining the crowded AI meeting-note race, as calendar-centric platforms race to absorb post-meeting work. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/calendly-throws-its-hat-into-meeting-note-taker-circus/)
