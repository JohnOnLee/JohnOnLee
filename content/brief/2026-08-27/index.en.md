## Startup / Product / Platform Radar
- **Z.ai ships GLM-5.3-Flash**: a MIT-licensed open-weight flash model (320B-A18B) with 1M context and multimodal input that approaches Claude Opus 4.8 on coding and agentic benchmarks at a far lower price. [Cloudflare](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-08-26-glm-5.3-flash-workers-ai/) · [explainx.ai](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/glm-5-3-flash-ox-alpha-official-launch-august-2026)
- **Alibaba open-sources Qwen3.8-Flash-Next**: an experimental open-weight MoE model (125B-A6B, only 6B active per token) that previews the next-generation Qwen4 architecture, released Aug 26. [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/08/26/alibabas-qwen-to-open-source-qwen3-8-flash-next-previewing-qwen4-architecture/) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next)
- **Korea's Wrtn raises $72M Series C**: the Seoul-based AI platform now values above $722M (over 1 trillion won), with its US-focused AI entertainment app OOC passing $7.2M in monthly revenue within three months of launch. [The Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/tech-science/20260826/wrtn-raises-72-mil-in-series-c-funding-round)
- **Self-driving truck startup Gatik raises $200M**: led by Qatar Investment Authority and Koch Disruptive Technologies, its largest round to date, bringing total funding to roughly $500M. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/self-driving-truck-startup-gatik-raises-200m-following-pepsico-deal/)
- **Fitbit co-founders launch Luffu Link, a $250 AI health band**: a screenless LTE wearable where background AI monitors activity, sleep and HRV, flagging changes and alerting family members. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/fitbit-founders-launch-luffu-link-an-lte-health-and-safety-band/)
