## Startup / Product / Platform Radar

- **Nvidia to back OpenAI's Ohio data center with up to $105B**: Nvidia will provide up to $105 billion in credit and hardware for OpenAI's Ports-Pike data center near Cincinnati, Ohio, and is investing $1.5 billion in SoftBank-linked developer SB Energy to become the facility's sole compute supplier. The campus could scale from an initial 4.25 GW to 8 GW. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-financing-open-ai-data-center-ohio.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-investing-1-5b-in-softbank-data-center-developer-behind-openai-project/) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-joins-ports-pike-project)

- **Wispr raises $280M at a $2B valuation**: The AI dictation startup raised a Menlo Ventures-led Series B, hitting a $2 billion valuation, and unveiled "Canto," a new model that cuts error rates from 30% to under 10%, as it expands beyond dictation into meeting note-taking. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/wispr-raises-280m-at-2b-valuation-as-it-looks-beyond-dictation/)

- **Higgsfield quadruples its valuation to $5.4B in eight months**: The AI image-and-video startup founded by ex-Snap exec Alex Mashrabov raised a $400M Series B led by DST Global, reporting $700M in annualized revenue, 30 million users, and 390 Fortune 500 customers. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/higgsfield-raises-400m-series-b-quadrupling-its-valuation-in-8-months-to-5-4b/)

- **Groq raises $350M to fund its chip-to-neocloud pivot**: The AI inference chip startup raised $350M led by Disruptive with Nvidia participation, but at a $3.5B valuation — down from $6.9B last September — as it shifts from building its own LPUs to operating Nvidia-GPU cloud infrastructure. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/groq-raises-350m-to-fuel-its-pivot-from-ai-chips-to-neocloud/)

## AI Future Signals

- **Anthropic's revenue run rate hits $65B**: Anthropic told investors its annualized revenue climbed to $65 billion in July, up roughly sevenfold from a year earlier — a signal that enterprise AI demand is still compounding. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/anthropic-says-annualized-revenue-climbed-to-65-billion-in-july.html)

- **Synchrony × OpenAI brings agents into the transaction layer**: Synchrony, the card issuer behind Amazon and Walmart, is partnering with OpenAI to integrate payments into ChatGPT shopping, with full integration expected in six to 12 months. AI agents are moving beyond discovery into actual checkout. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/synchrony-openai-chatgpt-shopping.html)

- **AI automation startup Relay shuts down as its CEO returns to Google**: Relay, which aimed to be the "new Zapier," is shutting down, and founder Jacob Bank is rejoining Google as VP of Product for Chrome — the latest sign of AI-agent talent consolidating back into big tech. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/ai-automation-startup-relay-shuts-down-staff-joins-googles-chrome-team/)

## Uncertainties / Keep Watching

- **Groq's down round raises an AI-infrastructure valuation-reset question**: A halved valuation is the price of Groq's chip-to-cloud pivot and may signal a broader correction in AI-infra startup valuations. Whether abandoning its own silicon for Nvidia dependence is durable remains unclear. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/groq-raises-350m-to-fuel-its-pivot-from-ai-chips-to-neocloud/)

- **Compute capex meets power and gas cost risk**: The 9.2 GW natural-gas plant behind OpenAI's data center will cost $33 billion, and gas-plant construction costs have risen 66% in two years — putting the compute buildout at risk of power and fuel cost inflation.
