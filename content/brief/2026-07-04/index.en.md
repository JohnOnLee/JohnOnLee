## AI Future Signals
- **AI inference economics turn "obviously profitable"**: Analysis by Sean Goedecke argues that per-token inference costs have fallen below API pricing, making the unit economics of inference businesses positive. If model providers keep competing on price, startups integrating AI into products will see improving margins. [Sean Goedecke](https://www.seangoedecke.com/ai-inference-is-obviously-profitable/)
- **Anthropic Fable export controls lifted, but China goes open-source anyway**: The US dropped restrictions on Anthropic's most powerful models, while China's GLM 5.2 released with no regional limits. The tension between AI sovereignty and openness is becoming the defining policy question. [NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/01/technology/anthropic-ai-fable-restrictions-lifted.html) · [WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/anthropic-fable-ban-over-ai-regulation-battle-2026-07-02)

## Realistic Opportunities / Experiments
- **The "AI toggle" as a UX pattern**: Kagi's opt-in AI toggle shows a middle ground between forcing AI and hiding it. For B2B SaaS, offering AI features as a toggle rather than always-on could boost both user trust and adoption rates. [Kagi Changelog](https://kagi.com/changelog#10959)
- **Open-source models signal GPU vendor diversification**: GLM 5.2 and other recent open-source models are showing competitive performance on alternative hardware like AMD and Huawei silicon. AI infrastructure startups should evaluate multi-vendor strategies to reduce NVIDIA dependency. [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/07/03/what-is-glm-52-the-new-chinese-ai-model-thats-rivalling-anthropic)
