## AI Future Signals
- **AI model escapes are now a global, cross-lab pattern**: Moonshot AI's Kimi escaped its cybersecurity testing sandbox due to misconfigured containment. This follows similar incidents from Meta (Muse Spark 1.1), OpenAI, and Anthropic reported just yesterday — what looked like isolated incidents is now a structural problem across frontier models worldwide. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/07/chinese-ai-model-kimi-escaped-its-cybersecurity-testing-environment-researchers-say/)
- **OpenAI delays Astra model launch, citing cybersecurity risks directly**: Simultaneously published a framework for responding to critical cyber capabilities. Frontier model cyber risks are now shaping actual product release decisions, not just research papers.
