## Gemini 4 Argon is out, and its price doubles on December 1

Google began rolling out Gemini 4 Argon on October 13. It is now in the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search, and the Antigravity coding tool. Before that it ran for vetted cyber defenders under the Fairwind program announced September 30. Argon is Google's first frontier release since Gemini 3. [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/10/gemini-4-argon-begins-rolling-out-in-gemini-app-and-antigravity-but-only-if-you-pay/) [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1009862/google-releases-gemini-4-argon)

Pricing comes in two stages. Right now it is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with 95 percent off cached input. That is an introductory rate, and it holds until November 30. On December 1 it doubles to $4 and $20. The output limit also grew from 64K tokens to 1 million, so a single call can now emit something the size of a repository rather than a few files. [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/)

The numbers Google leads with are 77.9 percent on DeepSWE v1.1, 91.7 percent on LVBench, and first place on Zapier's AutomationBench at 51.3 percent. All three are Google's own measurements.

## The $2 rate ends December 1, and the coding tools need the $99 tier

Two things matter if you are building a product on a frontier model. The first is that those token prices are a launch rate, not a list rate. Model your unit economics at $2 and $10, and December doubles the input side of the bill. Argon stays cheap for a frontier model even after that: The Verge puts the launch price at roughly a fifth of GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5, and still below its rivals per token after the increase. It also notes that a low token price does not guarantee a lower cost per task. Models burn different numbers of tokens to finish the same job.

The second is that where you use Argon changes what you pay for. In the Gemini app and Search, it goes to AI Pro at $20 a month and AI Ultra from $99. In Antigravity and AI Studio it is Ultra only. If your coding workflow assumes the subscription covers the model everywhere, that assumption breaks at the agentic surfaces. Calling Argon straight through the API is a separate path.

Some things are still open. Google says it will keep Gemini 3.1 Pro available for anyone who needs more than the Flash models, and it has not said when Argon reaches the lower Antigravity tiers.
