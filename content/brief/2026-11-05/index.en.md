## Hy4 keeps its list prices, while TokenHub's half-price offer has an end date

[Tencent launched Hy4 on November 4](https://www.tencent.com/tencent-launches-hy4-joining-the-ranks-of-the-worlds-leading-open-source-ai-models/). List prices match Preview, and Tencent Cloud TokenHub cuts them in half through December 31, which puts input at about US$0.90 per million tokens and output at US$2.70, with cached input near US$0.045.

Hy4 runs now on Tencent Cloud TokenHub, WorkBuddy, CodeBuddy, Yuanbao, WeCom's AI Assistant and ima. Third-party platforms such as OpenRouter serve it too. It adds visual understanding and computer use, both absent from Preview. WorkBuddy and CodeBuddy are free until November 15.

[OpenRouter's Hy4 endpoint](https://openrouter.ai/tencent/hy4) is Tencent's own. Context is 1,048,576 tokens, output caps at 65,536, and the listing shows US$0.417 per million input and US$1.2505 per million output.

Tencent ran the financial evaluation itself. It asked 213 financial professionals to judge 579 real-world tasks, and Hy4 scored 3.04 against 2.97 for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017. The weights are not out. Tencent says they and the system cards follow in the coming days, with no date.
